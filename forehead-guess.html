How to play
  1. Pick a deck. Hold the phone sideways on your forehead, screen facing your friends.
  2. Friends act, describe, or make sounds. No saying the word!
  3. Nod down (screen toward the floor) = got it ✅   Tip back (screen toward the ceiling) = pass ⏭️
Tip: on iPhone, turn on Rotation Lock so the screen doesn't flip mid-round. No tilt sensor? Tap the right side for correct, left side to pass.
